## question

### q-1
title: Capital of France
prompt: Name the capital city of France.
answer: Paris
notes: A classic warm-up prompt.

### q-2
title: First moon landing
guest prompt: Which country landed astronauts on the moon first?
answer: United States
notes: Good for a broad knowledge round.

## challenge

### c-1
title: Silent mime
guest prompt: Act out "a cat sneaking into a room" without speaking.

### c-2
title: Three-word challenge
guest prompt: Say the words "tiny", "dancing", and "robot" in order without laughing.

## rule

### r-1
title: No using the word "like"
prompt: You cannot say the word "like" for the next three turns.

### r-2
title: Reverse chair order
prompt: The turn order is reversed until the next spin.

### r-3
title: Finger-pistol til taler
prompt: Send finger-pistoler når nogen begynder at tale

### r-4
title: Ingen fyldord
prompt: Du må ikke sige fyldord som 'øh', 'uhm'

### r-5
title: West Side Story fingerknips
prompt: Du må ikke tale uden at lave fingerknips, "West Side Story"-style

### r-6
title: Høflig
